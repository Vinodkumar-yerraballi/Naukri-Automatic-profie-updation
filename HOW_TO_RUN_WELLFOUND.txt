WELLFOUND RESUME GUIDE
======================

Wellfound resume updates are not automated by this project. The resume flow
may be part of a specific job application rather than a general profile
resume field.

Sign in to Wellfound yourself, review your candidate profile, and upload or
attach the current data\resume.pdf where the site offers that option. Confirm
that the intended resume is selected before submitting an application.

The project does not automate Wellfound login, profile changes, or job
applications. Keep profile details accurate and do not rely on any tool to
guarantee interviews or shortlisting.
