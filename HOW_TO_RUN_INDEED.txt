INDEED RESUME GUIDE
===================

From the project directory in PowerShell, create a local Indeed session:

   .venv\Scripts\python.exe main.py --setup-indeed-session

Sign in yourself in the opened browser, complete any checks, and press Enter
in PowerShell. The local browser profile is saved in
data\indeed_browser_profile. Keep it private.

Replace data\resume.pdf with your updated text-based PDF. Run the normal
pipeline:

   .venv\Scripts\python.exe main.py

The app attempts an upload only when the resume has changed since the last
confirmed Indeed upload. Its state is tracked in
data\indeed_resume_upload.json. Indeed may redirect to a region-specific page
or change its profile interface. Check your Indeed profile after each attempt.
If the app cannot confirm success, verify the profile before retrying.

Indeed login and verification checks must be completed by you. This feature
uploads the resume only; skills, work experience, and projects are not
automatically filled on Indeed. It does not apply to jobs on Indeed and cannot
guarantee interviews or shortlisting.
